SMS Terms

Program. Ventura Energy Site Alerts is an automated text message program run by Ventura Energy LLC, 925 De La Vina Street, Ste 104, Santa Barbara, CA 93101.

What we send. By providing your mobile number on our sign-up form, you agree to receive recurring automated text alerts from Ventura Energy about grid power outages and power restoration at your site(s), including battery energy remaining and estimated pump run time. We do not send marketing messages through this program.

Frequency and cost. Message frequency varies with site events. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help. Reply STOP to any message to cancel; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at info@ventura.energy.

Consent is optional. Signing up is not a condition of any service from Ventura Energy.

Delivery. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Alerts depend on monitoring data from the site equipment and are not a substitute for on-site alarms.

Privacy. Participation is subject to our Privacy Policy.
