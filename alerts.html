Site alerts

Grid outage alerts by text

Ventura Energy runs battery storage at our customers' water pumping sites. If you work with one of those sites, you can get a text the moment we see grid power drop there, with the battery energy remaining and the estimated pump run time, and another alert when grid power is restored. Alerts come by email, and by text if you choose.

What you will get

  • An alert when grid power is lost at your site, with battery energy remaining and estimated pump run time.
  • An alert when grid power is restored.
  • By email, and by text only if you check the optional text-message box.
  • No marketing. Alerts are sent only when something happens at your site, typically a few per month.

Sign up

Text messages are optional. You can sign up for email alerts only. If you also check the text-message box on the form and enter your mobile number, you agree to receive recurring automated text alerts from Ventura Energy about grid power outages and power restoration at your site(s). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Signing up is not a condition of any service. SMS Terms · Privacy Policy

Each person signs up for their own phone. To stop alerts at any time, reply STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or email info@ventura.energy.
