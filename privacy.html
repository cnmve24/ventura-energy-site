Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Ventura Energy LLC collects through this website and our Site Alerts text program, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us by email, such as your name, company and email address.
  • For Site Alerts: your name, company, mobile number and the site(s) you want alerts for, from our sign-up form.

How we use it

We use contact details to reply to you and to work with your organization. We use Site Alerts sign-up information only to send the alerts you asked for and to handle STOP and HELP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers that process information on our behalf only to run our business, such as our email and form provider and our text messaging provider to deliver alerts. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep Site Alerts numbers until you opt out. Reply STOP to any alert, or email info@ventura.energy, and we will remove your number.

Questions about this policy: info@ventura.energy, or Ventura Energy LLC, 925 De La Vina Street, Ste 104, Santa Barbara, CA 93101.
